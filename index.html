Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Caffeine Intake does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on our servers. Drink logs, profile settings, and related information stay on your device.

We do not use accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

You can delete all app data from Settings in the app. Uninstalling the app also removes locally stored data.
